How the income is divided

In 2023, pre-tax income in the United States averaged $89,400 per adult. That total is not spread evenly. The bottom half of adults received 13.5% of it. The top 1% received 20.7%.

In 1963 the economy was smaller, $36,800 per adult in 2023 dollars, and it was divided differently. The bottom half received 19.0% and the top 1% received 13.0%. Fair Share keeps today's total fixed and changes only the division.

Seeing the top

The highest-income groups are too small to see on an ordinary chart. On the chart below, each of the four bands holds ten times fewer adults than the one before it, but every band gets the same width. That makes room to compare the top 0.1% with everyone else.
